

### Admin operations extension

The `/admin` route is a role-aware prototype console for the missing marketplace trust loop. It uses a human-review queue with protected document-preview states for professional license, Bar credential, and practice/language declaration. Reviewers can record approval, rejection, or correction requests; the UI explains that decisions record reviewer, timestamp and reason. Adjacent tabs cover scoped client support/disputes, safety and moderation reports, and Chapa payment/payout reconciliation. These are intentionally represented as local demo state until Authentication, Database, Storage, RBAC, audit logging, webhooks and real support workflows are connected.
